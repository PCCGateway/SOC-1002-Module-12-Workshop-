Mission 2 • Apply

Can You Use the Concepts?

Scenario A: A Growing Community

A community's population increases quickly. New housing developments appear outside the city. More residents commute by car, traffic increases, and undeveloped land becomes neighborhoods and shopping areas.

Scenario B: Neighborhood Change

A historically lower-income neighborhood attracts new businesses and higher-income residents. Buildings are renovated and property values increase. Some longtime renters say they can no longer afford their apartments.

Scenario C: Population & Resources

A rapidly growing city worries that its water system cannot meet future demand. One group argues that population growth must slow. Another says new technology can increase water supplies and reduce waste.

Mission 4 • Three Paradigms

Same Event. Three Sociological Lenses.

Now analyze the same current event three different ways. The point is not to choose the “correct” paradigm. Each lens asks different questions.

FUNCTIONALISM

Focus: functions, institutions, stability, disruption, interdependence.

CONFLICT THEORY

Focus: power, inequality, resources, winners and costs.

SYMBOLIC INTERACTIONISM

Focus: meaning, labels, language, identity, everyday interaction.
